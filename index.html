Why clean your bins?

  • No more smell. Rotting food residue is what makes bins stink, especially in summer.
  • Fewer flies and maggots. A clean bin gives pests nothing to breed in.
  • A healthier home. Sanitising reduces bacteria around the area where your family walks and plays.
  • Zero effort. We come to you, and you don't need to be home.
